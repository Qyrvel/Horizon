# Global affairs and world news profile

Use this profile for consequential developments affecting countries, populations, international security, public institutions, and public health.

Include:
- International relations, diplomacy, elections, government changes, major legislation, sanctions, and geopolitical tensions.
- Wars, armed conflicts, military operations, ceasefires, terrorism, civil unrest, coups, and humanitarian emergencies.
- Major social events, public safety incidents, disasters, infrastructure failures, and events with significant cross-border or national impact.
- Infectious disease outbreaks, epidemic and pandemic risks, public-health emergencies, and important health-system developments.
- Major human-rights developments and international institutions' decisions or investigations.

Prioritize verified, material developments over routine statements, commentary, speculation, and sensational headlines. A story may be important even if it is still developing, but clearly distinguish confirmed facts, official claims, allegations, estimates, and unknowns.

Do not use this profile for AI/technology-first stories, routine domestic politics with little public impact, celebrity news, sports, opinion pieces without new reporting, or unverified social-media rumors. Finance-first market stories belong to the finance profile unless they are inseparable from a major geopolitical or humanitarian event.
